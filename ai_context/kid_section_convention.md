When teaching a phase and writing/updating its `chapter/NN_*.md`, always append a section headed `# Explanation to a little kid` — a plain-English, analogy-first retelling of the same content (no jargon), ending with a "whole phase in six sentences" recap. The user requested this for chapters 2–6 in turn and it's now the house style.

**Why:** the user is a true beginner ([[teaching_style]]) and learns best from a simple analogy pass after the technical trace.

**How to apply:** after the technical chapter body, add the kid section; keep the main body precise/code-grounded and let the kid section carry the analogies (waiter-with-no-menu, GPS-for-meaning, amnesiac-with-notebook, chef-on-the-phone, sealed-envelope, etc.). Offer to append it rather than skipping it.
